Welcome

Welcome to Helvetic Business School in Switzerland.

Our aim is to provide a unique learning experience to our students that combines professional development with personal growth.

At HBS, we believe that creativity is key to success in business, and we encourage our students to think outside the box and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems. Our curriculum is designed to be challenging yet rewarding, and our experienced faculty members are dedicated to helping students reach their full potential.

Advantages

Why choose to study at HBS?

We pride ourselves on providing a unique and personalised training experience to our students.

01

A hands-on approach to business training

The training offered at HBS prepares you to be operational and work-ready from the get-go. Our teaching methods put a special emphasis on the transmission of practical skills and the acquisition of competencies — in practical terms, we provide you with toolboxes.

02

Personalised attention

Our small class sizes mean that you will receive personalised attention from our experienced professors, who are also seasoned practitioners. We believe in a supportive learning environment where students can interact closely with their professors.

03

Flexibility

We offer flexible schedules and tailored course offerings. This means that you can balance your academic studies with other commitments such as work or family obligations, and immerse yourself in Swiss culture.

04

Community and culture

Our small business school offers a strong sense of community and culture. We welcome students from diverse backgrounds and cultures, aiming to create a dynamic and inclusive learning environment.

Location

On the Swiss Riviera.

Our business school is located in La Tour-de-Peilz, a beautiful city with stunning scenery on Lake Geneva that provides a high quality of living and an equally rich culture.

The business school is also located near the cities of Geneva and Lausanne, which are large business hubs, providing ample opportunities for internships and networking with industry professionals.
