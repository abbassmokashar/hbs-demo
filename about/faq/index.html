Admissions and applications

Admissions and applications

HBS is based at Chemin du Levant 5, 1814 La Tour-de-Peilz, Switzerland — on Lake Geneva, between Lausanne and Montreux and under an hour from Geneva, which offers ample opportunities for internships and networking with industry professionals.

Click on the application link to start your application at HBS. Please note all information needs to be filled out in order to start processing your application. We will notify you if there is any missing information or documents.

Application Form: applicants must submit a completed and signed application form.

Photographs: six (6) passport-size photographs must be included with the application form submitted to HBS.

Recommendations: two (2) confidential letters of recommendation from instructors or employers familiar with the applicant’s past performance.

Résumé or CV: a brief written account of the applicant’s strengths and accomplishments.

Transcripts: official transcripts for all applicable academic work done at secondary or university-level institutions in the past years, with certified translations for documents not in English.

Motivation Letter: at least 500 words, telling the story of your academic development and explaining your reasons for applying.

Evidence of sufficient financial means: bank statement, sponsor letter, etc.

Language Proficiency: see the requirements page for accepted conditions and test scores.

Fees and finance

Fees and finance

After your application has been evaluated and approved, you will receive an offer letter where the deadline for the admission fee is stated. It is important to pay the admission fee before the stated deadline to prevent your application from being rejected.

All of your fees can be paid by bank transfer or online using your credit/debit card. More details regarding bank details and payment options can be found on your offer letter.

The full amount payable for your tuition fees is outlined on the offer letter. You can also check our Tuition and Fees page for the published amounts.

Yes. You can choose to pay either annually or in instalments.

Once we have received proof of your payment, the Admissions Office will contact you about what to do next.

Student visa and health insurance

Student visa and health insurance

Every student is required to have health insurance to study in Switzerland, which you will have to arrange once you arrive in Switzerland. HBS administration will inform and guide you through the process, and we already work with various health insurance providers you can choose from.

All students except EU/EFTA state members require a visa to enter Switzerland. After your application is accepted, the Office of Admissions will send you all the information and required documents to submit to the Consulate or Embassy of Switzerland in your country.

Please remember that the visa application process may take up to 3 months depending on the time of the year and the review procedure at your embassy or consulate. Plan accordingly.

Once you arrive in Switzerland, the Administration team will inform you about how to apply for your Swiss residence permit, which you can use to travel across Switzerland and the Schengen Area.

We can help provide the necessary documentation that can support your visa application. However, you will need to make an appointment and inquire about any other specific visa requirements with the embassy or consulate.

You will need to contact the embassy or consulate of Switzerland in your country, which you can find online. You can also contact us, and we will help you with any questions you may have.

Please note that we cannot influence or accelerate the process as it is independently processed by the Secretariat of Migration.

In the case of a rejected visa application, you can still appeal the decision. You should contact the HBS team to guide you through the process.

You will need to contact the embassy or consulate in your country to inquire about any required fees.

Foreign students can work up to 15 hours per week during their studies. However, students who are not from the EU/EFTA will need to wait 6 months after commencing their studies before they are allowed to work.

The exact date up to which you can stay in Switzerland will be set by the immigration authorities and will be outlined in your visa. You are not allowed to stay in Switzerland past that date.

Registration and enrollment

Registration and enrollment

The academic year begins in September and ends in July. HBS operates on a term system with three academic terms per year, and the source website has also described five academic terms and five intakes. Confirm the current structure and starting dates with the Admissions Office.

After you arrive in Switzerland, our administration team will go through the registration process with you at HBS.

You will be given your student ID during registration after arriving in Switzerland.

You will get your timetable 2 weeks before the starting date of each term.

All information will be given to you through the Student Guide, which you will receive before you arrive in Switzerland.

Arrival in Switzerland

Arrival in Switzerland

Ideally, you should arrive at least 1 week before the start of the term in order to accustom yourself and complete your registration process.

The nearest airport is Geneva airport, which is around an 80–85 minute train ride away. You will find trains directly to the region; depending on when you arrive, you might need to take a connecting train from Lausanne.

HBS is located at Chemin du Levant 5, 1814 La Tour-de-Peilz. The nearest regional train connections run along the Lake Geneva line between Lausanne and Montreux, which you can use to reach Lausanne, Montreux and Geneva.

Once you arrive in Switzerland, you will meet the Administration team, who will assist you with the registration process as well as assist you in obtaining your Swiss residence permit.
