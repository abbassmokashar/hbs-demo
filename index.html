About Helvetic Business School

A focused community with a global outlook.

HBS is a business school based on the Swiss Riviera in La Tour-de-Peilz. Our aim is to provide a unique learning experience that combines professional development with personal growth.

We believe that creativity is key to success in business. Students are encouraged to think beyond familiar answers and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems.

HBS fosters a dynamic and diverse community committed to excellence and impactful contributions to the world of business.

The HBS approach

Built around how people learn and lead.

Three principles shape every HBS classroom, from the first term to graduation.

01 / Skill-based education

Skill-based education

HBS aims at imparting tested effective working and problem-solving methods that remain invaluable throughout a career. Knowledge evolves, but practiced working methods are lifelong companions.

02 / Small class sizes

Small class sizes

Small class sizes ensure personalised attention, deeper learning and meaningful interaction with professors. The environment encourages collaboration and active participation.

03 / Support

Personalised support

Comprehensive support is offered throughout the learning journey, with personalised guidance and resources to help everyone succeed in their educational and career goals.

HBS × ISM dual degrees

One international journey. Two degrees.

Study in Switzerland and Germany, gain international exposure and hands-on experience, and graduate with two internationally recognised degrees.

Bachelor’s students complete Years 1 and 2 at HBS in La Tour-de-Peilz, then transfer to ISM in Germany for the final year. Master’s students complete one year at HBS followed by one and a half years at ISM.

HBS manages the transfer and all tuition is paid to HBS. Students choose from ten master’s specialisations across seven ISM campuses.

Admissions

Your next chapter, clearly mapped.

HBS operates on a rolling admissions basis. Applications, academic records and applicable fees should be submitted at least ten weeks before the start of each term.

01 / Apply

Apply

Submit the completed application form, all required documents and the CHF 150 application fee.

02 / Review

Review & interview

Once documents and fees are received, admissions evaluates the application and schedules an interview where applicable, reaching a decision within about ten business days.

03 / Offer

Offer letter

Accepted applicants receive an offer letter and student agreement setting the admission-fee deadline.

04 / Prepare

Enrollment & visa

Return the signed agreement and admission fee, receive the Certification of Studies and begin the visa process with HBS support.
