## Photography rules

- Maintain a simple asset register with source, usage permission, subject, date, and approved alt text.
- Never label regional Lake Geneva photography as the HBS campus.
- Do not use the old building, campus exterior, aerial campus plan, or exterior shots from the brochure.
- Review student images for consent before production use.
- Match faculty portraits to names before publication.

## Forms and personal data
