Overview

Leadership for a competitive global market.

Our goal at the Helvetic Business School is that our MBA students are fully prepared to lead and manage in today’s competitive global marketplace. Our curriculum is application based through experiential learning and focuses on the hard and soft skills business leaders will need in the Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR).

All courses are taught in English by leading business visionaries in small dynamic cohorts. All professors have experience in successful business ventures, providing our students with real-world lessons learned and best practices. Our professors share their challenges and successes with the students, giving them a front-line education.

The curriculum links cutting-edge business theory to practical application as students are guided to solve real-world challenges. This environment emphasises collaboration and team building alongside healthy competition.

At HBS, top-tier internationally diverse classmates come together, share professional experiences and form synergistic groups where mutual commitment and joint accountability lead to highly effective teams.

Beyond the depth and breadth of knowledge gained in the classroom, the lifelong connections developed while attending HBS will be of immeasurable value. To this end, we require and actively support internship placement, on-site company visits and executive mentoring.

With our vast network of contacts at nearby, world-renowned corporate headquarters, students are sure to find opportunities tailored to meet their specific needs. Whether the aspiration is to manage a leading company, have your own business or work for a family-run business, HBS provides the tools to follow the desired career path.

Our faculty-student ratio is roughly 1:7, and the small class size of approximately 20 students per class provides students with optimum personal interaction.

    Demonstrate a critical understanding of the major concepts and theories in the functional areas of general management, such as accounting, economics, finance and marketing.

  2. 02

    Cultivate crucial learning skills that will aid in seeking further studies in a preferred topic in an independent manner.

Dual degree with ISM in Germany

Two master’s degrees, one international route.

Earn two master’s degrees: a Master in Business Administration from HBS, and a choice of ten degrees from ISM in Germany.

Year 1 is spent at HBS in Switzerland covering core master’s courses in business, strategy and leadership, plus business consulting projects and case studies and an internship.

Year 2 transfers to ISM in Germany for advanced courses and company projects. The final half-year is spent at ISM writing and defending your Master’s thesis.

Program structure

Twelve weeks of study per term.

There are 12 weeks of study per term, plus two weeks of examinations. Examination periods usually occur in the middle and at the end of each term.

Holiday periods vary, but there is usually a four-week break in December, a break in Spring, and a longer break in July/August. Please refer to the Academic Calendar for details.

The Helvetic Business School operates on a term system with three academic terms per year. The academic year begins in September and ends in July. Fall Term runs September to December, Winter Term January to March, and Spring Term April to July.

A typical duration of study takes two years to complete. Each year consists of 3 terms. Students are expected to complete 60 ECTS (45 CH) credits per year. This amounts to six courses per term in order to complete their degree in two years.

The provision of specialisation modules in a particular term will depend on student requests and faculty availability; the listing of specialisation modules offered may change from year to year. All modules are normally for three credits in one term.

Graduate Degree Requirements

A clear path to graduation.

The conferring of an MBA degree from HBS follows only after completing the following requirements: academic credit requirements, academic performance requirements (cumulative GPA of 3.0), and attendance and residency requirements.

In addition, all outstanding accounts must be settled with the administration before graduation. Total CH to graduate is 90 CH and total ECTS to graduate is 120 ECTS.

Fees and Financial Policy

Payment of fees

Upon notification of acceptance, HBS will provide prospective students with an official Acceptance Letter, which is necessary in many countries for student visa requests as well as for military deferral. Prior to starting the visa application procedure, fifty percent (50%) of tuition and fees is payable, and tuition and fees must be paid in full at the time of admission.

The deposit will be credited toward the student’s tuition, or refunded if official notification of withdrawal is received thirty (30) days before enrollment. The balance of tuition and student fees is due by Admission Day at the beginning of each term.

Payments can be made by direct bank transfer. Please be sure the student’s name is included on the bank transfer notice. Non-payment will result in the student not being accepted in class.
