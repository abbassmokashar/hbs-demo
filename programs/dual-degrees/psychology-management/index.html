Overview

Earn a dual degree in Switzerland and Germany.

Blend advanced business leadership with behavioural insights in this transformative dual degree program.

Offered through Helvetic Business School in Switzerland and the International School of Management in Germany, this pathway combines a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from HBS with a Master of Science (MSc) in Psychology & Management from ISM.

It is ideal for those seeking leadership roles in HR, organizational development, behavioural consulting, or executive coaching.

Program Format

One year in Switzerland, then Germany

01 / HBS

Year 1 — HBS, Switzerland

MBA core subjects in strategy, leadership, finance, innovation, and global business, with real-world consulting simulations, leadership assessments, and team-based projects.

02 / Transfer

Year 2 — ISM, Germany

Specialised modules in organizational psychology, behavioural economics, leadership diagnostics, workplace communication, and talent management at the Dortmund campus, plus a Master’s thesis and an internship.

03 / Thesis

Final semester — ISM

Write and defend your Master’s thesis and complete an internship.

04 / Graduate

Two master’s degrees

Graduate with an MBA from HBS and a M.Sc. Psychology & Management from ISM.

Admissions Requirements

What you need to apply

  • A bachelor’s degree recognised in Germany (contact us to check whether your degree is recognised) or an equivalent qualification
  • English language proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL, or equivalent)
  • Motivation letter
  • CV or résumé
  • Transcripts and academic records
