Overview

A Swiss program with a global qualification.

Gain a competitive edge with a Certificate in Market Research from Helvetic Business School. Crafted for professionals, this Swiss-based program — incorporating The Market Research Society (MRS) Certificate in Market Research — enhances your understanding of consumer behaviour, industry trends and data-driven decision-making.

This certification combines the rigour of Swiss standards with the opportunity to achieve a globally recognised qualification from MRS, ensuring an unmatched level of expertise.

MRS is the UK professional body for research, insight and analytics. We recognise 5,000 individual members and over 500 accredited Company Partners in over 50 countries who are committed to delivering outstanding insight. As the regulator, MRS promotes the highest professional standards throughout the sector via the MRS Code of Conduct. MRS supports the sector with specialist training and qualifications, professional membership, company accreditation, cutting-edge conferences, glittering awards and advice on best practice.

Program highlights

What is included

01

Comprehensive online training

Online training towards the MRS Certificate qualification, including the Foundation Course in Market Research developed by the Market Research Society in London.

02

MRS Certificate in Market Research

Successfully complete a multiple-choice online exam to achieve the globally recognised MRS Certificate qualification.

03

Guided research project

Put your skills into action with an applied project mentored by a field expert.

04

Exclusive conference access

Join an online academic conference organised by the American Institute of Applied Sciences in Switzerland (AUS).

05

Dual certification

Gain an MRS Certificate in Market Research and an HBS certificate, with 4 ECTS credits and an AUS participation certificate.

06

Flexible timing

Start anytime. Complete the MRS online training and exam within 120 days, with an additional six months to finish your project and join the conference.
