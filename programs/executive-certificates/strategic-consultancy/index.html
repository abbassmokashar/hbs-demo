Overview

Drive impact where it matters.

Looking to sharpen your consultancy skills and expand your influence in the private sector? This executive online certificate equips you with the tools, strategies and network to lead impactful projects in private sector development (PSD).

Developed by the globally recognised Maastricht School of Management, this program blends cutting-edge research with hands-on consultancy training to help professionals shape the future of business and development.

Advantages

Why this executive certificate sets you apart

  • Build specialised consultancy and research skills for private sector development
  • Work with real-world case studies and learn from global development experts
  • Connect with leaders from donor agencies, governments, NGOs and the private sector
  • Join interactive online workshops with thought leaders from around the world
  • Become part of a global alumni network dedicated to PSD and international consultancy

Designed for professionals who shape policy and business

What you’ll learn

Three modules

  1. Module 1

    Foundations and Research in Private Sector Development

    Learn the core concepts of PSD and master qualitative and quantitative research methods. Includes an interactive stakeholder workshop with guest experts.

  2. Module 2

    Consulting Skills and Professional Strategy

    Explore advanced consultancy techniques, ethics in development work, and career strategy for impact-focused professionals. Engage in a live workshop with top consultants and government representatives.

  3. Module 3

    Policy Design, Project Management, and Influence

    Develop effective policies, manage stakeholder networks, and communicate research that drives decisions. Wrap up with a final capstone consulting project and high-level stakeholder workshop.

Schedule

September 2025 to March 2026.

Study online at your own pace with bi-weekly modules, live Zoom sessions and workshop-style interactions.

Outcomes

What you’ll gain

  • A deep understanding of PSD frameworks and consultancy approaches
  • The ability to design and implement effective strategies for development and reform
  • A valuable international network of professionals, donors and decision-makers
  • Access to future research and project opportunities through the MSM alumni network
  • An executive-level certificate recognised across sectors and borders

How to apply

To join the next intake

Simply fill out the online application on MSM’s platform. You’ll need:

  • A bachelor’s degree and at least 3–5 years of relevant professional experience
  • A strong command of English (C1/C2 level)
  • A completed online form with your CV and a copy of your passport or EU ID
