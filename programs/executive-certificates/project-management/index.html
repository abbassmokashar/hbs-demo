Overview

Deliver projects that make a difference.

Want to lead projects with precision, purpose and global impact? This executive certificate in Project Management equips you with the tools and techniques to design, implement and evaluate projects of any scale.

Developed with Maastricht School of Management, this flexible online or blended program is tailored for professionals who want to elevate their project leadership and strategic execution skills.

Advantages

Why this executive certificate sets you apart

  • Master the full project cycle from planning and execution to evaluation
  • Apply globally recognised tools like Critical Path, PERT and Logic Models
  • Gain practical experience through real-life case studies and weekly assignments
  • Access personal coaching to align the course with your professional challenges
  • Learn online at your own pace — or opt for in-person networking in Maastricht

Who should enroll

Designed for project leaders

01

Project leaders and coordinators

Project leaders, team members and coordinators managing development or business projects.

02

Consultants

Consultants engaged in project-based work across sectors.

03

Trainers and lecturers

Trainers or lecturers in the field of project or program management.

What you’ll learn

Modules and formats

  1. Module 1

    Project Foundations and Analytical Thinking

    Understand project life cycles, logic models and stakeholder analysis. Learn to frame project goals using results-based approaches.

  2. Module 2

    Planning, Execution and Monitoring Tools

    Explore key planning methods including cost scheduling, PERT and critical path. Master monitoring and risk management with tools for communication and reporting.

  3. Fully Online

    Format and duration

    8 weeks, self-paced (6 hours per week), with individual coaching sessions.

  4. Blended

    Format and duration

    8 weeks online plus a 5-day campus-based immersion in Maastricht.

Outcomes

What you’ll gain

  • Strong analytical and strategic planning skills
  • The ability to deliver complex projects on time and within budget
  • Real-world frameworks to manage risk and stakeholder dynamics
  • Personalised coaching and support
  • A professional certificate recognised across sectors

Your certificate

Certification and requirements

MSM

Certificate of Participation

Upon successful completion, participants receive a Certificate of Participation issued by Maastricht School of Management, aligned with EQF Level 6 or 7.

Content

Details included

The certificate includes your name, course title and the program components.

Requirements

Study load and attendance

A minimum of 14 hours study load and 80% attendance is required.
