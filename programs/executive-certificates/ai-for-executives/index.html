Overview

Harness the power of AI to lead digital transformation.

AI is no longer optional — it is essential. This executive certificate equips leaders with a strategic understanding of artificial intelligence and how to leverage it for innovation, efficiency and growth.

Designed by MSM for decision-makers, the program bridges the gap between technology and business leadership, giving you the confidence to guide your organisation through AI-driven transformation.

Advantages

Why this executive certificate sets you apart

  • Gain a strategic overview of AI, including generative AI, machine learning and neural networks
  • Learn how to integrate AI into your business models and operations
  • Explore global case studies and apply AI frameworks to your own challenges
  • Engage in interactive workshops and project-based learning
  • Receive expert guidance from AI thought leaders and digital transformation consultants

Who should enroll

Designed for decision-makers

01

Business leaders and executives

Executives driving innovation within their organisations.

02

Managers

Managers responsible for strategic planning and digital transformation.

03

Decision-makers

Decision-makers seeking to integrate AI into real-world business processes.

04

Innovation leads

Professionals looking to lead AI-powered innovation in competitive markets.

What you’ll learn

Four modules

  1. Module 1

    AI Fundamentals and Strategic Insight

    Develop a foundation in key AI technologies including machine learning, neural networks and generative AI.

  2. Module 2

    Strategic AI Integration

    Understand how AI can be embedded into your organisation’s strategy to boost efficiency and decision-making.

  3. Module 3

    Design Thinking and AI-Driven Innovation

    Apply design thinking frameworks to develop innovative AI-powered products and services.

  4. Module 4

    Real-World Case Studies and Implementation

    Explore how industry leaders are using AI to solve problems, scale operations and drive results.

Schedule

An intensive five-day program.

Offered online or in class, this intensive short course includes expert-led lectures, project workshops and group discussions.

Outcomes

What you’ll gain

  • A leadership mindset to guide AI-driven transformation
  • Tools to strategically assess and implement AI within your business
  • Exposure to global best practices and innovation frameworks
  • Personalised insight from industry leaders and case study analysis
  • A recognised executive certificate validating your advanced skillset

Your certificate

Certification and requirements

MSM

Certificate of Participation

Upon successful completion, you’ll receive a Certificate of Participation from Maastricht School of Management, aligned with EQF Level 6 or 7. The certificate outlines your course achievements and confirms your participation in a post-graduate training.

Attendance

80% required

Attendance of 80% is required to receive the certificate.

How to apply

To secure your spot

Submit your application through the MSM platform. You’ll need:

  • A bachelor’s degree and 3–5 years of relevant work experience
  • Proficiency in English (C1/C2 level)
  • A completed online application form with your CV and ID/passport
